PvZ PSP 0.18.1 - asset-free preview installation

This download contains software and preparation tools. It does not contain the
original game's resources. Follow ASSET-FREE-SETUP.txt to prepare resources
from your own supported disc dump on your computer.

Back up an existing PSP/GAME/PVZPSP folder and its saves before updating.
Copy the ZIP's PSP/GAME/PVZPSP folder to the PSP memory card. Place your locally
prepared resources in that folder under the name data:

  PSP/GAME/PVZPSP/EBOOT.PBP
  PSP/GAME/PVZPSP/data/...

Keep existing player saves and preferences. Start the game from the PSP Game
menu. Custom firmware/homebrew support is required.

PSP-1000 defaults to 30 FPS. Open the gear flower / Options, highlight Frame
rate, then press Right for 60 FPS (test), Left for 30 FPS, or X to toggle.
The setting applies immediately and is saved. Press Select during gameplay
to display measured FPS. Return to 30 FPS if needed.

Street/2000/3000 retain their usual 60 FPS target. Unknown model queries remain
at 30 FPS. Simulation stays at 60 updates per second. Physical PSP-1000 speed,
Memory Stick stalls and long-session behavior remain unverified by emulator
tests. See PERFORMANCE-0.18.1.md.

Newly prepared resources use GPU-ready PVA4 graphics. The new program still
accepts older PVA3 graphics, but misses the optimization. The old 0.18 program
cannot read PVA4; downgrading requires restoring its old program AND old data.

No player saves, preferences or benchmark flags are supplied. Wireless co-op
and versus remain unfinished and unavailable.
