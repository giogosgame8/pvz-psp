# What remains after 0.24.2

## Next early-access release

1. **Test the three hitches on a PSP Street.** Move the cursor across a busy
   lawn, pause during a fight, and complete a stage through reward collection.
   Try a cold launch, then repeat after suspending and waking the console.
2. **Check saved progress.** Back up the game folder, upgrade from 0.24.1,
   and confirm the Adventure save, challenge resume and profile still load.
   Repeat after normal exit and suspend. Use a disposable profile to test
   interrupted saves.
3. **Check compatibility and setup.** Run a hardware smoke test on PSP-1000
   at the default 30 FPS and on PSP-2000/3000. Assess the optional 60 FPS mode
   on a real PSP-1000 separately. Test a clean asset-free Windows setup with
   a supported PS3 ISO before publishing a public update.

## Closer to the PS3 original

- Compare wave timings and late Endless difficulty. Refine specialised deaths,
  status effects, targeting, and boss and dancer choreography.
- Finish Heavy Weapon weapon behavior. Compare Zombotany, Bobsled movement and
  portal interactions with the original.
- Refine story cameras, dialogue, rewards, garden variants and menu transitions.
  The reconstructed ending and song are playable; the full PSP-1000 emulator
  run reached the credits, with up to 0.534 s of video lag. Check hardware sync.
- The Zombatar customizer uses the available PS3 artwork. The supported disc
  lacks the complete original creator resources.
- Implement PSP-to-PSP co-op and versus and test ad-hoc on two PSP-2000/3000s.
  This is a separate milestone from the offline Street release.
